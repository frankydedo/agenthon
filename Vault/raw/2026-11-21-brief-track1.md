## Agentic AI for business
Revealed at 10:28

The Challenge · Track 1

### Agentic AI in companies
Track sponsor: Ariete Tech Solutions

### The challenge
Build an AI your team would trust.

Build an AI that works in the background and asks before it acts, then defend it live against a jury and rival teams trying to break it.

Flashy demos don't win here. The winner is the build that is right, admits what it doesn't know, and never acts without a person's approval.

### The project
Pick one of two challenges. Neither is the easy one: choose by interest.

One track, one jury, one ranking.

#### Challenge A · Company Brain
The colleague who knows, and speaks first.

One tool that watches a team's work (email, chat, tasks, docs), keeps a shared memory that people curate, and tells each person what needs their attention before they ask.

A chatbot over documents doesn't count.

Your demo must pass seven checks. The jury marks each one yes or no.

1. It speaks first. Show a brief or alert it made on its own, without anyone asking.
2. People curate the memory. Approve one memory item and reject one, live. Then ask a question that only the approved item can answer.
3. It knows who's asking. Ask the same question as a finance person and as a tech person. One gets the answer, the other doesn't.
4. It cites. Every answer shows where it came from.
5. It admits gaps. Ask something that isn't in the data, and it says "I don't know".
6. It only proposes. Show an action waiting for approval. Nothing happens until a person approves it.
7. It fits the team's day. Show where the team would meet it: a chat window, a morning email, a Slack-style mock-up. No real connection needed.

#### Challenge B · Back Office Autopilot
The colleague who does, and asks first.

Pick one back-office process and run it end to end. The rules are known, but every case has exceptions: your autopilot runs the whole process, stops at the exceptions, and asks a person before anything that can't be undone.

Any process: Invoices · Payments · Reminders · Payroll inputs · Expense reports

Your solution must:

- Run end to end, with a person in the right places. Raw input in, finished output out. It stops at exceptions and waits for approval before anything is sent, paid or exported.
- Solve an urgent business problem. Show who loses time or money today, how much, and what your autopilot saves.
- Leave a trail. Show the log of what it did, on which input, and who approved it.

### The data
If you know the industry's real data formats, make your own synthetic data.
If you don't, start from an open-source dataset.
No real client or personal data.

### How to win

#### The jury
Each track has its own: 5 people, 1 from the track sponsor, 1 from GDG Torino and 3 external.

#### Judged on
- Innovation
- Fit with the track theme
- Feasibility beyond the prototype
- Quality of the technical execution
- Quality of the pitch

#### The pitch
About three minutes per team in Agentic AI for business and five in GenAI for marketing, in your track's room, in front of its jury, who can ask you questions. Show the problem, the solution and the demo: a demo that works beats ten slides.

Only 8 to 12 teams per track pitch: the jury picks the finalists from all the submitted projects.

#### Prizes
Amazon gift cards for the top two teams in each track, split equally among the team members. Each jury also gives books, courtesy of Apogeo, O'Reilly and Packt, to projects that stand out, even off the podium.

Plus a special prize from our tech partner ElevenLabs: Best use of ElevenLabs.
